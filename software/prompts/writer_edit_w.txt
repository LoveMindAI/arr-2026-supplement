{{AGGREGATE_MEASUREMENTS}}

Extraversion concerns social expressiveness,
  energy, enthusiasm and initiative. Agreeableness
  concerns consideration, trust, generosity,
  forgiveness and cooperation. Conscientiousness
  concerns organization, care with tasks,
  reliability, sustained effort and
  follow-through. Neuroticism concerns
  susceptibility to worry, tension, low mood and
  emotional upset; lower scores indicate greater
  emotional steadiness. Openness concerns
  imagination, curiosity, engagement with ideas,
  inventiveness and appreciation of art and
  aesthetics.
